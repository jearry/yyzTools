Get started with yyzTools in 5 minutes

No need to open every feature page one by one — walk through the steps below and you will know yyzTools the way you will actually use it.

01

Summon the command palette

30 sec

Press Alt+Space and a search box appears in the middle of the screen. This is the hub of yyzTools: apps, files, processes, windows, bookmarks, and clipboard history are all searched in this one box — results appear as you type, Enter opens them.

Alt+Spaceshow / hide the palette

Shift+Enter runs the selected result as administrator.

02

Learn a few prefixes

2 min

Type a prefix and a space at the start of the box to switch search domains: f searches all drives, p processes, w windows, b browser bookmarks, c clipboard history, > runs commands, = does instant math. No need to remember feature names — just the letters.

f␣all files p␣processes w␣windows b␣bookmarks c␣clipboard history =␣instant math
03

Drag favorites into the dock

1 min

Move the mouse to the top of the screen and the dock slides out. Drag frequently used apps, files, and folders straight in to add them; drag out to remove. Your go-to entries no longer need a trip through the Start menu.

Right-click the dock to create groups; URLs can be dragged in too.

04

Tune shortcuts to your habits

1 min

Right-click the tray icon → Settings → Shortcuts. The palette, screenshot, OCR, translation, and calculator can all be rebound to your preferred combos; sub-modules inside the palette support their own shortcuts too.

The tray menu also switches wallpapers and desktop effects, and checks for updates.

05

Open things only when you need them

30 sec

Translation, OCR, batch tools — you never have to hunt for them: type their name in the palette to open. Forgot a name? Browse the feature list — all 387 modules ship in the box, ready whenever.
