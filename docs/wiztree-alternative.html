Who's eating your disk — and how to clear it

yyzTools' Disk Space Analyzer scans a drive and draws a colored treemap of folders by size, the same technique WizTree and WinDirStat use, fast even across a million-plus files. The difference: it isn't a lone utility — from the very files that hog space you can reveal, preview and send to the Recycle Bin, then jump straight into file search and cleanup.

When WizTree or TreeSize is still the better pick

If you want a pure specialist for disk analysis — the absolute fastest re-scan, report printing and export, scanning network shares and non-NTFS or removable volumes — WizTree and TreeSize are more mature and more complete for that single job. yyzTools does not aim to be the deepest standalone analyzer.

When yyzTools is the better fit

If your real question is "why is my C: drive full, and how do I fix it without installing another program," yyzTools answers both at once: scan to a treemap, then act on those files — reveal, recycle, search, and run the file cleaner — inside the same free toolbox that already gives you a launcher, OCR and translation.

Common questions

Is yyzTools' analyzer faster than WizTree?

Both read the NTFS $MFT, so scanning a large volume takes seconds, not minutes, either way. WizTree is built solely for this and can be faster and more mature on extreme scans and re-scans; yyzTools wins on what happens after the scan, not on shaving a fraction of a second off it.

I already have WizTree or WinDirStat — do I need this?

If all you want is to see usage, keep them; they are excellent. Reach for yyzTools when you are tired of one app to see space, another to clean junk, another to find files — and would rather fold those into one.

Can it scan network drives or USB sticks?

It focuses on fixed local NTFS volumes; network shares and non-NTFS or removable media have limited support today — a real gap versus WizTree and TreeSize.
