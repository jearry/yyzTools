Common questions

Is Raycast free on Windows?

Its core is: launcher, Clipboard History (3 months), Snippets, Quicklinks, Calculator and thousands of extensions are free. Pro ($10/month) adds the AI layer, Translator, Cloud Sync, custom themes and unlimited history. yyzTools charges nothing for any of its 387 modules, including translation and OCR.

Does yyzTools need an account like Raycast?

No. yyzTools has no account at all — settings, clipboard history and indexes live on your machine. Raycast signs in with a Raycast account (third-party reviews note this; its FAQ states core features work offline after sign-in).

Both open with Alt+Space — do they clash?

Yes, the default global hotkey is Alt+Space on both. Change it on either side the first time you run them together (yyzTools: tray icon → Settings → Shortcuts); after that they coexist.
