Looking for a DeepL alternative?

DeepL is the quality benchmark for neural translation — and its best parts sit behind a paid Pro subscription. Youdao (有道) is China's dictionary-first translator, ad-supported with a VIP upsell. yyzTools takes a third route: a free, accountless translator living in the same Alt+Space toolbox as your launcher, full-disk search and OCR clipboard. You won't get DeepL's whole-document formatting or Youdao's authoritative dictionaries; you will get one app that captures, reads and translates a screen region, then keeps the result searchable.

When DeepL or Youdao is the better pick

If translation is your craft, the specialists win. DeepL's neural output is the quality bar — especially across European languages — and its whole-document translation and Write are worth the Pro subscription. Youdao's authoritative English–Chinese dictionaries, offline packs and polished word-capture are the go-to for Chinese learners. If that is what you need, reach for them.

When yyzTools is the better fit

If you want solid machine translation that is simply free — for business too — with no account, no API key and no upgrade popups, and you'd rather it sit beside your launcher, full-disk search and OCR clipboard than be one more standalone app. A captured region flows screenshot → text → translation → searchable clipboard without ever leaving the toolbox.

Common questions

Is yyzTools as good as DeepL?

For raw machine-translation quality — especially European-language prose and formatted documents — DeepL is the stronger tool, and its best features are paid. yyzTools targets a different need: free, accountless translation of the text you paste, type or capture, bundled inside a toolbox rather than sold as a premium translation service.

Can it translate a screenshot or a whole document?

A screenshot, yes: capture a region and yyzTools runs OCR, then translates the recognized text in one step. A whole .docx or .pptx with formatting kept, no — that is DeepL's specialty, and Youdao does document translation too. yyzTools handles the text you paste, type or capture.

Do I need an account, or will it nag me to upgrade?

No account and no API key are needed to translate. yyzTools is free for personal and commercial use with no upgrade popups — where DeepL gates its best features behind a Pro subscription and Youdao pushes a VIP membership.
